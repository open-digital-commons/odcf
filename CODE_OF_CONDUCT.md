# Code of Conduct

ODCF is a conversation about how people can share a public space fairly. That conversation should itself be a good public space.

## We expect

- **Good faith.** Assume others want this to work, even when they disagree with you.
- **Arguing with ideas, not people.** Criticise proposals as hard as you like; do not attack the people who make them.
- **Patience across difference.** Many contributors write in a second or third language, and come from very different professions and political traditions.
- **Honesty about interests.** Disclose if you represent a government, company, party or funder (see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)).
- **Making room.** Notice who is not speaking, and invite them in.

## We do not accept

- harassment, threats, or personal attacks;
- discriminatory language or behaviour of any kind;
- publishing others' private information;
- deliberate disruption, spam or coordinated manipulation of discussions;
- sexual content or advances.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainers. Until a dedicated contact address exists, contact the convener through [afifsay.org](https://afifsay.org). Reports will be handled confidentially.

## Consequences

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently remove participants who breach this code. Decisions and their general reasons will be recorded, without identifying people who reported.
