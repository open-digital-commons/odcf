# Open Digital Commons Framework (ODCF)

**A public call to imagine, design and build social media that belongs to the people who use it.**

> Status: **Draft v0.2. Open for discussion.** Nothing here is decided. Everything here can be improved, challenged or replaced.

---

## The problem, in one paragraph

A handful of private companies now own the places where billions of people talk, argue, organise, learn and grieve. These platforms treat our conversations, relationships and creative work as raw material to be mined for profit. Their algorithms are secret. Their rules change overnight. Their ownership can change hands in a single transaction. No democratic society would accept this arrangement for its roads, its water or its public squares, yet we have accepted it for the most important public square of our age.

## The idea

What if social media were treated as **public digital infrastructure**:

- **owned by people**: your content, your identity and your connections belong to you, and you can take them anywhere;
- **working for people**: no surveillance advertising, no secret algorithms, and you choose how your feed works;
- **governed by people**: decisions made through democratic, transparent processes that include ordinary citizens;
- **protected by democratic states, but not controlled by them**: funded and legally safeguarded by a consortium of willing governments, the way member states fund CERN without directing its science.

## Where this fits

ODCF is not alone, and does not want to be. Others are already working toward the same goal:

- a **European Citizens' Initiative** asking the EU to create a public, independent social network (registered March 2026);
- **policy proposals** for a publicly funded "digital public space" built on open protocols, such as those from Open Future;
- **civil-society coalitions** asking governments to fund the Fediverse and other open networks;
- **builders** of open, independent infrastructure, such as Eurosky, Free Our Feeds, and the Fediverse and AT Protocol communities.

What these efforts largely share is a call for public, open, independent social infrastructure. What is still missing is a worked-out answer to the hardest question: **how would such a commons actually be governed, funded and kept independent**, including from the very governments that fund it?

**That is ODCF's focus: an open, collaboratively drafted governance charter for a public digital commons.** It is meant to support the efforts above, not compete with them. See [RFC 001, §5](RFC-001.md#5-the-wider-movement-and-where-odcf-fits).

## What this repository is (and is not)

This is **not** a product, a company, a rival campaign, or one person's project. It is an **open drafting space**.

The document at the centre, [RFC 001](RFC-001.md), sets out a problem, a set of principles, a starting draft charter, and a long list of [open questions](OPEN-QUESTIONS.md). Some basic questions, such as *should public money build something new, or strengthen and govern open networks that already exist?*, are deliberately left open. We believe they should be answered by many people, not by the person who started the conversation.

The model is Linux, the web, and Wikipedia: things that became great because nobody owned them and everybody could improve them.

## How you can help

You do not need to be a programmer. We need:

| If you are… | You could… |
|---|---|
| A citizen who cares about this | Read RFC 001, tell us what's missing, share it |
| A lawyer or policy expert | Help design the legal structure → [WG 1](working-groups/WG1-policy-law.md) |
| A software architect or developer | Help shape the technical architecture → [WG 2](working-groups/WG2-protocols-architecture.md) |
| A researcher in democracy, moderation or online safety | Help design governance and safety → [WG 3](working-groups/WG3-deliberation-safety.md) |
| An economist or public-finance expert | Help design funding that can't be captured → [WG 4](working-groups/WG4-economics-funding.md) |
| A translator | Help bring this to more people → [Translations](translations/README.md) |
| Part of an NGO, university or public body | Tell us whether this is useful and what would make it more so |
| Already working on a related initiative | Tell us what you need from a governance charter, and what we've got wrong |

Start with [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
