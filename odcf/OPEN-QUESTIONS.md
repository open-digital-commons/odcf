# Open Questions

This is the living list of questions ODCF needs answered. Each one should have a matching **issue** in this repository, where discussion happens. If you think a question is missing, open a new issue.

Labels: 🟢 good place to start · 🔵 needs specialist knowledge · 🔴 foundational (affects everything else)

## Strategy
- 🔴 **Q1.** Build new, adopt and govern existing open networks, or create a protocol-neutral commons layer? (RFC 001, §6)
- 🔴 **Q2.** Should ODCF aim to *replace* commercial platforms, or to offer a public alternative that coexists with them?
- 🟢 **Q3.** Which existing open projects should we talk to first, and how do we approach them as partners rather than competitors?
- 🟢 **Q4.** What does "success" look like in 1, 5 and 10 years?

## Independence and the role of states (WG 1, WG 4)
- 🔴 **Q5.** How can states fund and legally protect the infrastructure without gaining any control over it? (RFC 001, §7)
- 🔵 **Q6.** Which legal form fits best: international non-profit association, foundation, federation of national bodies, or treaty organisation?
- 🔵 **Q7.** What happens when a member state demands removal of content that is legal elsewhere?
- 🟢 **Q8.** Which states or regions are realistic first partners, and why?
- 🔵 **Q9.** How is non-state participation (cities, universities, NGOs, foundations) structured?

## Technology (WG 2)
- 🔵 **Q10.** What is the minimum set of open standards for identity, content and social graph portability?
- 🔵 **Q11.** How should an "open algorithm marketplace" work in practice: who can publish ranking algorithms, and how are they audited?
- 🔵 **Q12.** How is the infrastructure distributed across jurisdictions so no single state can switch it off?
- 🟢 **Q13.** What should a non-technical user's first experience be?

## Governance and safety (WG 3)
- 🔴 **Q14.** How is the Citizen Assembly selected, how large is it, how long do members serve, and what real powers does it have?
- 🔵 **Q15.** How are moderation decisions made at scale, across languages and cultures, and how do appeals work?
- 🔵 **Q16.** How do we protect against coordinated manipulation, harassment and illegal content without central surveillance?
- 🟢 **Q17.** How do we include people who are not online much, not technical, or not in Europe and North America?

## Funding (WG 4)
- 🔵 **Q18.** What would this realistically cost to run at different scales?
- 🔵 **Q19.** Which funding sources are compatible with independence (digital services tax share, research programmes, multi-year state contributions, donations, membership)?
- 🔵 **Q20.** Should any revenue be allowed beyond public funding and donations? Under what limits?

## The charter (all WGs)
- 🟢 **Q21.** Draft Articles IV–XI of the starting charter (RFC 001, §9).
- 🔵 **Q22.** Which principles, if any, should be unamendable?
- 🔵 **Q23.** What happens to users, data and code if the institution fails or is captured?

## This project itself
- 🟢 **Q24.** Is "Open Digital Commons Framework" a good name? Is there a better one that works across languages?
- 🟢 **Q25.** Which civil-society organisations should be invited to review RFC 001?
