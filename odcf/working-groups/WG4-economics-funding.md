# WG 4: Economics, Funding and Anti-Capture Safeguards

**Status:** Looking for founding members and a coordinator

## Purpose
Work out how public digital infrastructure can be funded stably and transparently, in a way that protects its independence.

## Key questions
- What would this realistically cost to run at different scales (pilot, national, continental)? (Q18)
- Which funding sources are compatible with independence: a share of digital services taxes, research and innovation programmes, multi-year state contributions, donations, membership? (Q19)
- How can funding be structured (formulas, multi-year commitments, multiple funders) so no single funder gains leverage? (Q5)
- Should any revenue be allowed beyond public funding and donations? Under what limits? (Q20)
- What happens financially if a major funder withdraws?

## First deliverables
1. A rough **cost model** for running social infrastructure at three scales, based on public data from existing open projects where available.
2. A **funding options map**: each source, its likely size, its stability, and its risks to independence.
3. A first draft of **Article VIII** of the charter (funding rules and safeguards against capture).
4. Lessons from how public broadcasters, research infrastructures and open-source foundations are funded.

## Useful backgrounds
Public finance, economics, non-profit management, tax policy, infrastructure operations, grant-making, cooperative economics.
